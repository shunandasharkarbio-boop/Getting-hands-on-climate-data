## 📈 Yearly Average Temperature Analysis

To investigate long-term climate change in Paris, the **yearly mean temperature** was calculated for the period **1950–2019**. Annual averages were generated by grouping daily temperature observations by year and computing the mean temperature for each year. The results were then visualized using a **scatter plot with connected lines**, allowing long-term temperature trends to be observed.

The resulting plot shows a **gradual warming trend** over the 70-year period. Although the increase in annual mean temperature appears modest, it represents a statistically significant long-term rise in the climate of Paris, consistent with observed patterns of global climate change.
