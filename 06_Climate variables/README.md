
# 🌍 Climate Variables

## 📖 Overview

While **temperature** is one of the most commonly discussed aspects of climate, it alone cannot fully describe Earth's climate system. Scientists monitor a wide range of environmental measurements, known as **Climate Variables**, to better understand climate variability and long-term climate change.

These standardized measurements are called **Essential Climate Variables (ECVs)**.

---

# 🌱 Essential Climate Variables (ECVs)

**Essential Climate Variables (ECVs)** are a set of key physical, chemical, and biological variables used to systematically observe and monitor changes in Earth's climate.

The definitions and standards for ECVs are maintained by the **Global Climate Observing System (GCOS)**.

GCOS is jointly sponsored by:

- 🌍 World Meteorological Organization (WMO)
- 🌊 Intergovernmental Oceanographic Commission of UNESCO (IOC–UNESCO)
- 🌿 United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP)
- 🔬 International Science Council (ISC)

These organizations coordinate global climate observations across the **atmosphere**, **land**, and **oceans**, providing guidance for improving climate monitoring worldwide.

> **Currently, GCOS defines 55 Essential Climate Variables (ECVs).**

## 💧 Why is Water Vapor Important?

Water vapor describes the **humidity of the air near Earth's surface** and plays a critical role in both weather and climate.

It influences:

- 🌡️ Human comfort and health
- 🐄 Livestock and wildlife well-being
- 🦟 Insect activity and swarming behavior
- 🌱 Plant growth and disease occurrence
- 💦 Evaporation from land and water surfaces
- ☁️ Cloud formation
- 🌧️ The hydrological (water) cycle
- ☀️ Earth's energy balance and climate feedback mechanisms

Since evaporation supplies water vapor to the atmosphere, it directly affects cloud formation, precipitation, and the exchange of energy between Earth's surface and the atmosphere.

---

## 🌍 Why Monitor Essential Climate Variables?

Monitoring ECVs helps scientists to:

- Detect long-term climate change
- Improve weather and climate predictions
- Understand interactions between the atmosphere, land, and oceans
- Support climate research and environmental policy
- Assess the impacts of climate change on ecosystems and society
